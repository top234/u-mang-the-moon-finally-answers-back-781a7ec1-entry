One line, in the voice of the Moon replying at last to the wishes, howls, and late-night promises of everyone staring up at it. After four billion years of listening, the Moon finally answers — and the answer is a flat, tired no.

> "After four billion years of wishes, howls, and 2 a.m. promises, my answer is: no, I won't make them text you back."

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the line to `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`). The program validates the line first: one line, 1–240 characters, no leading or trailing whitespace, not only whitespace.
